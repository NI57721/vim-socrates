*socrates* *vim-socrates*


A Vim/Neovim plugin for typing the Ancient Greek characters.

INSTALLATION                                                  *socrates-install*

You can use your preferred plugin manager.

>
    " dein.vim
    call dein#add('NI57721/vim-socrates')

    " vim-plug
    Plug 'NI57721/vim-socrates'

    " neobundle
    NeoBundle 'NI57721/vim-socrates'

    " jetpack
    Jetpack 'NI57721/vim-socrates'

    " packer
    packer.startup(function() use 'NI57721/vim-socrates' end)
<

USAGE                                                           *socrates-usage*

Add one of the following three lines to your vimrc or init.vim. You can then
use it to toggle between your current keymap and the selected Socrates keymap.
My favourite is |socrates-greed|. To learn more about each keymap, see
|socrates-keymaps| and |socrates-key-mappings|.
>
    nnoremap <silent> <leader>q <Plug>(socrates)
    nnoremap <silent> <leader>q <Plug>(socrates-latin)
    nnoremap <silent> <leader>q <Plug>(socrates-greed)
<

KEYMAPS                                                       *socrates-keymaps*

The following features are common to all keymaps.~

  Diacritics~

  You can enter diacritics after typing the keys corresponding to a Greek
  letter. You can combine the diacritics in any order, provided that the
  resulting combination is valid in Ancient Greek. For example, each of `a~[_`,
  `a~_[`, `a_[~`, and the other possible orders produces `<char-0x1F87>` (ᾇ).

    `[`   - Rough breathing mark,    as in `ἁ`.
    `]`   - Smooth breathing mark,   as in `ἀ`.
    `   - Grave accesnt mark,      as in ὰ.
    `'`   - Acute accesnt mark,      as in `ά`.
    `~`   - Circumflex accesnt mark, as in `ᾶ`.
    `_`   - Iota subscript/adscript, as in `ᾳ`, and `ᾼ`.
    `-`   - Macron,                  as in `ᾱ`.
    `^`   - Breve,                   as in `ᾰ`.
    `"`   - Diaeresis,               as in `ϋ`.

  Punctuations and additional characters~

  You can also enter Ancient Greek punctuation marks and several characters
  that are rarely used today.

    `?`   - Greek question mark,        `;`.
          Note that this is not a semicolon.
    `;`   - Greek ano telia,            `·`.
    `:`   - Greek ano telia,            `·`.

    `F\`  - Capital digamma,            `Ϝ`.
    `f\`  - Small digamma,              `ϝ`.
    `M\`  - Capital san,                `Ϻ`.
    `m\`  - Small san,                  `ϻ`.
    `Q\`  - Capital koppa,              `Ϙ`.
    `q\`  - Small koppa,                `ϙ`.
    `H\`  - Capital heta,               `Ͱ`.
    `h\`  - Small heta,                 `ͱ`.
    `S\`  - Capital Archaic sampi,      `Ͳ`.
    `s\`  - Small Archaic sampi,        `ͳ`.
    `Z\`  - Sampi,                      `Ϡ`.
    `z\`  - Small sampi,                `ϡ`.
    `SH\` - Capital sho,                `Ϸ`.
    `Sh\` - Capital sho,                `Ϸ`.
    `sh\` - Small sho,                  `ϸ`.
    `W\`  - Capital Pamphylian digamma, `Ͷ`.
    `w\`  - Small Pamphylian digamma,   `ͷ`.
    `ST\` - Stigma,                     `Ϛ`.
    `St\` - Stigma,                     `Ϛ`.
    `st\` - Small stigma,               `ϛ`.
    `j\`  - Yot,                        `ϳ`.


  Separating slash~

  To avoid ambiguity when converting input into Ancient Greek characters,
  vim-socrates provides the separating slash `/`. It allows you to confirm the
  conversion of the preceding input.

  For example, suppose you want to enter `τη`, but your input is converted into
  `θ`. In that case, you can enter `t/h` instead to produce `τη`.

VARIABLES                                                   *socrates-variables*

g:socrates_smart_mode                                    *g:socrates_smart_mode*
    Enables smart mode.
    It is set to `v:true` by default.
    To learn more about smart mode, see |socrates-smart-mode|.

g:socrates_punctuations                                *g:socrates_punctuations*
    Specifies the Greek punctuation marks used in |socrates-smart-mode|.
    Its default value is shown below.
    ",.'\";:? \<char-0x037E>\<char-0x0387>\<char-0x00FF>"



==============================================================================
KEY MAPPINGS                                             *socrates-key-mappings*

There are key mappings to toggle the value of |&keymap|.

*<Plug>(socrates)*
    Toggles between the current |&keymap| and |socrates|.

*<Plug>(socrates-latin)*
    Toggles between the current |&keymap| and |socrates-latin|.

*<Plug>(socrates-greed)*
    Toggles between the current |&keymap| and |socrates-greed|.

To learn more about each keymap, see |socrates-keymaps|.



==============================================================================
SMART MODE                                                 *socrates-smart-mode*

When smart mode is enabled, certain characters are automatically replaced with
other characters in accordance with Ancient Greek grammar as you type in
Insert mode.

  Sigma detection~

  When you type a punctuation mark after a lowercase sigma, or leave Insert
  mode immediately after typing one, the sigma is replaced with a final sigma.
  For the list of recognised punctuation marks, see |g:socrates_punctuations|.

  Rho detection~

  When you type a rho at the beginning of a word, it is replaced with a rho
  bearing a rough breathing mark.
